NestAdmin

A desktop administrative assistant for Windows, built for small businesses.

NestAdmin reads the email and documents your business receives (invoices, PDFs and scanned documents), extracts and validates their data and prepares reply drafts for a person to review. It is installed on your own computer. Documents, OCR, credentials and working data stay on it; AI analysis is done by an external provider that you choose (by default Ollama cloud, from Ollama Inc.) or by optional local AI.

What it does

  • Reads documents: plain text, PDFs with text and scanned PDFs. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your computer.
  • Extracts and checks invoice data: supplier, dates, amounts and taxes. It checks the arithmetic, flags missing data and possible duplicates, and never makes values up.
  • Works with your email: if you connect a Gmail or Microsoft 365 account, it reads the messages in your inbox and prepares a reply draft in the same thread.
  • You decide what gets sent: a draft is only sent after you review and approve it, or when a specific rule that you created and enabled covers it. NestAdmin does not delete email.
  • Visible history: every decision and action is recorded in the app.

Privacy by design

  • Runs on your computer. The publisher of NestAdmin has no servers that receive, store or process your email or documents.
  • External AI by default, with your key and your confirmation. NestAdmin proposes Ollama cloud as the AI provider. It sends nothing until you save your own key, pick the model and confirm the destination the text will go to (Ollama Inc., mainly in the US). It then runs a test with synthetic text, without your data. You can choose another provider (OpenAI-compatible or Claude) or local AI.
  • What leaves your computer. Only the text the task needs: the text already extracted on your computer from the invoice (OCR and PDF reading are always local) or, for an email, its subject, sender, recipients, date and body, together with the instructions. The original file, images, the database, your credentials and your paths are not sent.
  • Optional local AI. You can install local AI (Qwen3 8B, via Ollama) so the text does not leave your computer, or use it as a fallback if the external provider does not respond. NestAdmin never switches to another external provider on its own.
  • Protected credentials. Google and Microsoft authorizations are obtained with OAuth 2.0 from the app and stored in Windows Credential Manager.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Gmail access

When you connect a Gmail account, NestAdmin requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope, which it uses only to read your messages, create reply drafts in the thread and send a draft when you approve it or a rule you enabled covers it. It does not delete email. You can disconnect the account in the app or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • 16 GB of RAM recommended only if you install the optional local AI.
  • Internet connection to install, for the external AI provider and to work with email accounts.

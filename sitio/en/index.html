NestAdmin

A desktop administrative assistant for Windows, built for small businesses.

NestAdmin reads the email and documents your business receives (invoices, PDFs and scanned documents), extracts and validates their data and prepares reply drafts for a person to review. It is installed on your own computer and, by default, all processing happens there.

What it does

  • Reads documents: plain text, PDFs with text and scanned PDFs. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your computer.
  • Extracts and checks invoice data: supplier, dates, amounts and taxes. It checks the arithmetic, flags missing data and possible duplicates, and never makes values up.
  • Works with your email: if you connect a Gmail or Microsoft 365 account, it reads the messages in your inbox and prepares a reply draft in the same thread.
  • You decide what gets sent: a draft is only sent after you review and approve it, or when a specific rule that you created and enabled covers it. NestAdmin does not delete email.
  • Visible history: every decision and action is recorded in the app.

Privacy by design

  • Runs on your computer. The publisher of NestAdmin has no servers that receive, store or process your email or documents.
  • Local AI by default. The language model (Qwen3 8B, via Ollama) runs on your own computer.
  • External AI only if you choose it. You can explicitly choose Groq as the provider, with your own key. Only then is the processed text sent to Groq. NestAdmin never switches providers on its own.
  • Protected credentials. Google and Microsoft authorizations are obtained with OAuth 2.0 from the app and stored in Windows Credential Manager.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Gmail access

When you connect a Gmail account, NestAdmin requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope, which it uses only to read your messages, create reply drafts in the thread and send a draft when you approve it or a rule you enabled covers it. It does not delete email. You can disconnect the account in the app or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • 16 GB of RAM recommended for local AI.
  • Internet connection for the initial installation and to work with email accounts.

Support

Contact

Email us at [CORREO]@humannaia.com. Include your Windows version and describe the problem.

We will never ask for your password, tokens or API key. Do not send them by email. Avoid attaching real emails or documents with personal data; if an example is needed, use a made-up one.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • 16 GB of RAM recommended for local AI.
  • Internet connection during installation, which downloads Ollama and the language model if they are not already on the computer.

Connecting an email account

In the app, choose Gmail or Microsoft 365 and sign in. Google's or Microsoft's official page opens in your browser, where you will see the permissions NestAdmin requests and can accept or decline them. NestAdmin does not see your password.

Reviewing and sending replies

NestAdmin prepares a reply draft within the message thread. The draft is not sent until you review and approve it, unless you created and enabled a specific rule for that case. Every action is recorded in the app's history.

Disconnecting an account or revoking access

  • Disconnect the account in the app: its tokens are removed from Windows Credential Manager.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Microsoft, personal account: account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Microsoft, work or school account: myapps.microsoft.com or your organization's administrator.

Deleting your data from the computer

Uninstalling NestAdmin keeps your data so you don't lose your work. To delete it, after uninstalling, remove the %LOCALAPPDATA%\NestAdmin folder. Ollama and the language model are kept because other apps may use them; you can uninstall them from Windows settings.

AI provider

By default, NestAdmin uses local AI. If you choose Groq, the processed text is sent to Groq with your key. If the local AI fails, NestAdmin tells you and does not switch to Groq on its own.
